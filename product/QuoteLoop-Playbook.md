# QuoteLoop Starter Playbook

## The 15-minute weekly routine

1. Add every open quote to the board immediately after sending it.
2. Pick a next follow-up date while the job is still fresh.
3. On the due list, choose one channel and send one useful, human message.
4. Record the answer: won, lost, paused, or move the date.
5. Every Friday, review open quoted value and the reason for lost work.

## A calm follow-up sequence

**After 1–2 days**

> Hi — just checking you received the quote for [job]. Happy to answer any questions or clarify the options.

**After 5–7 days**

> Hi [name], I’m planning the next few weeks and wanted to check whether you’d like me to hold a slot for [job]. No pressure if plans have changed.

**After 14 days**

> Hi [name], I’ll close this quote for now so I don’t keep bothering you. If you’d like to revisit [job] later, just reply and I’ll pick it back up.

## Rules that protect trust

- Edit the script so it sounds like you.
- Never invent urgency, scarcity, reviews, qualifications or a deadline.
- Stop when a customer says no or asks not to be contacted.
- Follow the channel’s rules and your own legal/privacy obligations.
- A follow-up is a service check-in, not permission to pressure someone.

## Review metrics

Track weekly: open quotes, due quotes completed, replies, won/lost, quoted value won, and time spent. The goal is not maximum messages; it is fewer forgotten opportunities and better customer experience.
