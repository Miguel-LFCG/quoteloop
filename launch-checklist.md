# QuoteLoop launch checklist

## One-time founder actions

1. Review the copy, offer and the free board at `https://quoteloop.mycuba.xyz` after deployment.
2. Decide whether the £49 founding setup offer is genuinely deliverable within 14 days. If not, change the scope before taking payment.
3. Create a payment link in the payment provider of your choice **only after approving the offer**. Set the amount to £49, make it a one-off payment, and put the link behind the pilot approval step. Do not publish a payment link until refunds and delivery terms are written.
4. Set a private `ADMIN_TOKEN` for lead review; never put it in Git or browser code.
5. Personally review and reply to every pilot application. This is an approval-gated pilot, not automated outreach.

## First customer fulfilment

- Ask for trade, current quoting method, quote volume, channels and desired follow-up cadence.
- Configure the free board with the customer's first 5–10 real quotes on their own device, with their permission.
- Give them the playbook and a 15-minute handover.
- After 14 days, review replies/wins and ask whether the workflow earned its keep.

## Safety gates

No mass outreach, scraped personal data, automated messages, payment collection, contracts, or customer-domain deployment is enabled by this repository.
