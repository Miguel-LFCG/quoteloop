# QuoteLoop launch and validation plan

## Offer and monetisation

### Free acquisition product

**QuoteLoop Free Board — £0**

- Local-first quote list; no account.
- Due-today, overdue, all and open views.
- Ready-to-edit phone, email and WhatsApp wording.
- JSON backup/import.
- No automatic sending and no customer data uploaded.

### First paid offer

**Founding Setup — £49 one-off**

For one trade business, up to 10 initial quotes, one configured workflow, trade-specific scripts, a simple weekly routine and 14 days of async implementation help. No payment is collected on the site until the founder confirms fit and delivery scope.

### Scale path, only after validation

- £9–£19/month hosted workspace with cloud backup and reminders, if at least 3 pilot users ask for it and at least 2 would prepay.
- £49–£99/month for a small team only after support and data separation are proven.
- Possible partner channel with independent bookkeepers, trade accountants or web designers; no affiliate commitments until retention exists.

## Landing copy

**Headline:** Stop losing jobs you already quoted.

**Subhead:** QuoteLoop turns open quotes into a short, calm list of who needs a call, WhatsApp or email today—so follow-up happens before the next job takes over.

**CTA:** Try the free board.

**Paid CTA:** Request founding setup.

**Proof framing:** Do not publish unverified testimonials, invented customer counts or guaranteed win-rate claims. Use the existing direct product evidence and say clearly where the calculator is illustrative.

## First distribution opportunities (manual, not spam)

1. **Trade-specific communities:** Join relevant UK electrician, plumber, builder, decorator and landscaper groups/forums. Read rules first. Publish a genuinely useful post such as “I made a free quote follow-up board that runs in your browser; here are 3 polite follow-up scripts.” Do not mass-DM or scrape members.
2. **Founder-led 1:1 interviews:** Invite 20 independent trades businesses to a 15-minute workflow interview. The ask is for feedback, not a sales blast. Target businesses with public business contact channels and keep a do-not-contact list.
3. **Accountant/bookkeeper partners:** Offer a free co-branded checklist for their trade clients; ask whether missed quotes/admin are a recurring issue before proposing a referral arrangement.
4. **Search-led free content:** Publish the existing playbook and calculator under queries such as “how to follow up a quote UK”, “quote follow up template tradesman” and “how to track quotes without a CRM”. Keep all advice honest and practical.
5. **Personalised mini-audits:** For a handful of businesses that invite feedback, review their publicly visible enquiry path and send one short observation plus the free tool. No claims that they have no website, no scraped personal addresses and no automated sending.

## 7-day first-customer plan

### Day 1 — publish and instrument
- Review the live landing page and app.
- Confirm `ADMIN_TOKEN` is private.
- Create an approved £49 payment link only if delivery/refund wording is ready; initially keep it private and take payment after fit review.
- Create a simple interview tracker with: business, trade, source, contacted?, reply, trial started, paid, outcome.

### Day 2 — ten conversations
- Contact ten personally selected UK trades businesses or community members through permitted professional channels.
- Message: “I built a tiny no-login board to stop open quotes disappearing in WhatsApp/email. I’m looking for 15 minutes of blunt feedback; no pitch unless useful. Would you test it with five old quotes?”

### Day 3 — five more conversations + observe
- Ask where quotes are currently recorded, how many are open, and what happens after no reply.
- Do not lead with the calculator or a revenue promise; lead with their workflow.

### Day 4 — guided trials
- Offer 3–5 qualified people the free board. Watch them add five quotes. Record friction and objections.
- Fix only blockers that stop the core action.

### Day 5 — paid fit review
- For users who returned or report an active leak, offer the £49 setup: configure the routine, scripts and first quote list.
- State scope, delivery date, cancellation/refund policy and that it is founder-led.

### Day 6 — fulfil first paid setup
- Take payment only through a provider link the founder has reviewed.
- Deliver the setup and ask the customer to use it for seven days.

### Day 7 — review evidence
- Count conversations, replies, trials, quotes added, return visits, paid setups and objections.
- Decide: continue only if people use the board and at least one qualified customer pays; otherwise revise the problem/offer before adding features.

## 30-day prove/disprove plan

### Week 1: problem and activation
- 20–30 relevant conversations.
- 5 guided trials.
- Activation target: 60% of trials add ≥5 quotes within 48 hours.
- Kill signal: nobody can name a current place where open quotes are being lost.

### Week 2: retention and payment
- 10 active trials.
- Return target: ≥40% return on day 7.
- Payment target: 2 paid £49 setups or 5 explicit prepayment-qualified requests for a hosted version.
- Kill signal: users like the idea but will not put real quotes into it.

### Week 3: repeatable acquisition
- Publish 2 useful posts and 1 practical guide.
- Test two channels: trade communities and accountant/bookkeeper conversations.
- Target: ≥5 qualified applications and at least 1 paid setup without paid ads.

### Week 4: economics and roadmap
- Deliver all paid setups and collect a short outcome interview.
- Calculate acquisition time, delivery time, revenue, refunds and support load.
- Continue if: ≥3 paid setups, ≥50% of trial users return in week 2, and median delivery time is ≤90 minutes.
- Improve if: usage is strong but willingness to pay is weak; change packaging or niche, not the core workflow.
- Kill/pivot if: <1 paid setup after 30 qualified trials, or users consistently say existing tools/spreadsheets are good enough.

## Metrics

- Acquisition: qualified conversations, source, landing visits, calculator runs, pilot applications.
- Activation: app opens, `quote_added`, 5-quote activation rate, export/import usage.
- Engagement: day-7 return, `followup_script_copied`, due quotes completed, statuses updated.
- Commercial: applications → paid setup, revenue, refund rate, delivery minutes, support minutes.
- Product value: customer-reported forgotten quotes prevented, replies, won/lost quotes and quoted value—not guaranteed revenue.

The live app records only aggregate activation events; quote contents remain in the visitor’s browser. A local watchdog checks for new pilot applications every five minutes and alerts the founder without automatically replying.

## External action boundary

This build does not send outreach, accept payments, buy a domain, create a contract or deploy customer sites. The founder must approve the offer, create a payment link, review the legal/privacy wording and manually approve every first customer.
