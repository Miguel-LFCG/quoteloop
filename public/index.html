Straight answers

Questions a busy tradesperson might ask.

Is this another CRM?

No. The first version is intentionally a small follow-up board. It does not replace your accounting, scheduling or job-management system.

Does it send messages automatically?

No. It prepares an editable message and opens your chosen channel. Sending stays manual until there is explicit consent and a proven workflow.

Where is my data stored?

The free board stores its quote workspace locally in your browser. Export the JSON backup from the app. Pilot applications are stored on this site only so we can reply.

What happens after I apply?

We ask a few practical questions, confirm whether the workflow fits, and agree the scope before any payment. There is no automatic charge.
