Pilot privacy note

What happens when you apply.

What we collect

If you submit the pilot form, QuoteLoop stores the name, trade, business email and optional workflow note you provide. The free quote board does not upload your customer quotes; those stay in your browser unless you export them.

Why we collect it

We use the pilot details only to review fit, reply to your request and arrange the founder-led setup if you choose to continue. We do not sell the information or use it for mass marketing.

How long we keep it

Pilot applications are kept only while the pilot is being evaluated and fulfilled, then deleted when no longer needed. You can ask for a copy or deletion through the same pilot conversation.

Important pre-launch note

This is a small founder pilot, not a finished legal entity or regulated service. Before paid promotion or wider launch, the operator must add a named business contact, confirm the retention period and review the applicable UK data-protection obligations.
