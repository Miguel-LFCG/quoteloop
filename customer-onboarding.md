# QuoteLoop customer onboarding

## 15-minute setup

1. Open the free board on the device you use for work.
2. Add five current or recently sent quotes. For each one, choose the next follow-up date while the details are fresh.
3. Pick the channel the customer already prefers: phone, WhatsApp or email.
4. Use the short script as a starting point; edit it so it sounds like you.
5. Mark the outcome after each reply: won, lost, paused or move the follow-up date.
6. Export a JSON backup before changing device or browser.

## Your first week

- Check Today each morning or when you finish a job.
- Follow up only the due items you can genuinely serve.
- Record what happened rather than guessing.
- Do not invent urgency, reviews, availability or guarantees.
- Stop when the customer says no or asks not to be contacted.
